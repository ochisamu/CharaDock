## Product Hunt draft

Name: CharaDock

Tagline: An AI companion you can work with, not just talk to

Short description:

Meet your desktop co-worker. CharaDock combines expressive characters, voice
conversation, and Codex-powered work. Share a screenshot, explain what you want,
and review the result together. Customize your companion and extend it with
Skills and MCP tools.

Maker comment:

I wanted an AI that felt like someone working beside me—not a chat tab I only
open when I need an answer.

CharaDock puts an expressive companion on your desktop. In this demo, I show
Kohaku a broken layout and ask for help. GPT-6 Astra handles the work through
Codex; Kohaku keeps the conversation connected to what is actually happening.

The character isn't there to announce an invented success. It should help you
understand the work, see the result, and decide what to do next.

What would you want to work on with a desktop companion?

## Claims checklist

- Say “Astra” only for a successfully verified Astra run.
- Say “screenshot” for a single captured image, not “sees everything live.”
- Don't imply unlimited autonomous access to the computer.
- Local speech options and local character rendering do not mean model
  inference is offline. Codex requests use the connected service.
- Don't promise free OpenAI usage or universal account/model availability.
- Don't imply endorsement by OpenAI, Microsoft, or Product Hunt.
- Keep external links in a follow-up post if the main post includes the video.
- ESP32 hardware footage is optional; only include it if the physical device
  and its actual audio are captured, not a mock animation.
